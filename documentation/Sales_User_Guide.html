Sales Management

Complete Guide to Managing Sales Processes and Customer Relationships

Overview

The Sales Management module provides comprehensive tools for managing the entire sales lifecycle, from lead generation to deal closure. Users can track prospects, manage opportunities, forecast revenue, create quotes and proposals, and maintain detailed customer relationship records.

This module integrates seamlessly with customer management, invoicing, and reporting systems to provide a complete sales management solution.

Create a New Lead

Steps to Add a Lead

  1. Navigate to Sales → Leads → New Lead as shown in the below image.
    ![Create Lead](images/sales/lead_create.png)
  2. Fill out the following details in the form that appears:
    [Screenshot: Lead creation form]
  1. After Creating the Lead
    Click Create. You will be redirected to the View Lead page. Here, you can:
    • Edit Lead: Modify lead details.
    • Convert to Opportunity: Create sales opportunity from qualified lead.
    • Activities: Log calls, emails, and meetings.

Create Sales Opportunity

Steps to Convert Lead to Opportunity

  1. From a qualified lead record, click Convert to Opportunity.
    ![Convert Opportunity](images/sales/opportunity_convert.png)
  2. Complete the opportunity details form.
    ![Opportunity Form](images/sales/opportunity_form.png)
  3. Click Create to create the sales opportunity.

💼 Opportunity Details

Opportunity Name
Descriptive name for the sales opportunity.
Account
Link to the customer account.
Expected Revenue
Estimated revenue value of the opportunity.
Probability (%)
Likelihood of closing the deal (0-100%).
Close Date
Expected closing date.
Sales Stage
Current pipeline stage:
  • Prospecting
  • Discovery
  • Proposal
  • Negotiation
  • Closed Won
  • Closed Lost
Products/Services
Specific products or services being proposed. Used for inventory planning and sales analysis.
Competition
Known competitors involved in this opportunity. Helps with competitive positioning and strategy.
Decision Makers
Key people involved in the buying decision. Include their roles and influence level.

Sales Activities and Follow-ups

Logging Sales Activities

  1. From a lead or opportunity record, click + Add Activity.
    [Screenshot: Activity logging interface]
  2. Select activity type and enter details.
    [Screenshot: Activity entry form]
  3. Click Save to record the activity.

Activity Types

Activity Type Purpose Typical Information
Phone Call Voice communication with prospect Call duration, discussion topics, next steps
Email Written correspondence Subject, key points, attachments sent
Meeting In-person or virtual meetings Meeting duration, attendees, agenda covered, outcomes
Proposal Sent Formal proposal submission Proposal details, value, submission method
Demo/Presentation Product demonstration Demo type, features shown, feedback received

Scheduling Follow-ups

Follow-up Date
Next scheduled contact date with the prospect. Creates automatic reminders.
Follow-up Type
Planned activity type (call, email, meeting, proposal review).
Notes
Context for the follow-up, key topics to discuss, or information to prepare.

Quotes and Proposals

Creating Sales Quotes

  1. From an opportunity record, click Create Quote.
    [Screenshot: Quote creation interface]
  2. Add products/services and configure pricing.
    [Screenshot: Quote builder with product selection]
  3. Review quote totals and terms.
  4. Generate PDF and send to customer.

Quote Components

Quote Number
Unique identifier for the quote. Auto-generated but can be customized to match company numbering systems.
Valid Until Date
Quote expiration date. Creates urgency and protects against price changes.
Line Items
Individual products or services included in the quote:
  • Product/Service name and description
  • Quantity required
  • Unit price and total line amount
  • Discount percentage (if applicable)
Terms and Conditions
Payment terms, delivery conditions, warranty information, and legal disclaimers.
Quote Status
Current status:
  • Draft: Quote being prepared, not yet sent
  • Sent: Delivered to customer awaiting response
  • Accepted: Customer approved the quote
  • Rejected: Customer declined the quote
  • Expired: Quote passed validity date

Sales Pipeline Management

Pipeline Overview

The sales pipeline provides a visual representation of all active opportunities in various stages:

Pipeline Stage Typical Actions Expected Outcomes
Prospecting Research, cold outreach, lead qualification Initial interest confirmed, requirements identified
Discovery Needs analysis, stakeholder identification, budget discussion Clear understanding of customer needs and decision process
Proposal Solution design, pricing, formal proposal presentation Customer feedback on proposed solution and pricing
Negotiation Contract terms, pricing adjustments, implementation planning Agreement on all terms and conditions
Closed Won Contract signing, implementation handoff Revenue recognized, customer onboarding initiated

Moving Opportunities Through Stages

  1. Review opportunity progress and customer feedback.
  2. Update opportunity stage when criteria for next stage are met.
  3. Adjust probability percentage based on stage and customer engagement.
  4. Update expected close date if timeline changes.
  5. Log activities and next steps for continued progression.

Sales Reporting and Analytics

Key Sales Reports

Report Type Purpose Key Metrics
Sales Forecast Revenue prediction and planning Weighted pipeline value, expected close dates, confidence levels
Sales Performance Individual and team productivity Activities logged, opportunities created, deals closed, revenue generated
Conversion Analysis Pipeline efficiency measurement Lead-to-opportunity conversion, stage progression rates, time in each stage
Lost Deal Analysis Identify improvement opportunities Loss reasons, competitor analysis, stage where deals are lost

Generating Sales Reports

  1. Navigate to Sales → Reports.
  2. Select the desired report type and time period.
  3. Choose filtering options (sales rep, product, region).
  4. Generate report in PDF or Excel format.
  5. Schedule automated reports for regular distribution.

Use Cases & Examples

B2B Software Sales Process

Technology company selling business software solutions:

  • Lead generation: Website forms, trade shows, referral programs
  • Lead qualification: Budget, authority, need, timing (BANT) criteria
  • Discovery calls: Understand current systems, pain points, requirements
  • Solution demos: Customized product demonstrations addressing specific needs
  • Proposal: Detailed implementation plan, pricing tiers, support packages
  • Negotiation: License terms, implementation timeline, training requirements
  • Closure: Contract execution, implementation handoff to delivery team
Manufacturing Equipment Sales

Industrial equipment manufacturer managing complex sales cycles:

  • Lead identification: Industry targeting, engineering specifications review
  • Technical consultation: Site visits, requirement analysis, feasibility studies
  • Custom configuration: Equipment specifications, installation requirements
  • Proposal development: Technical specifications, delivery timeline, financing options
  • Stakeholder management: Engineering, procurement, finance, operations teams
  • Contract negotiation: Payment terms, delivery milestones, warranty coverage
  • Project handoff: Manufacturing, installation, training, support
Professional Services Sales

Consulting firm selling advisory and implementation services:

  • Relationship building: Executive networking, thought leadership content
  • Opportunity identification: Business challenges, strategic initiatives
  • Needs assessment: Current state analysis, desired outcomes, constraints
  • Proposal presentation: Methodology, team qualifications, project timeline
  • Scope refinement: Statement of work, deliverables, success criteria
  • Contract execution: Master agreement, project-specific work orders
  • Project initiation: Team assignment, kickoff meeting, project planning

Best Practices

  • Consistent Data Entry: Maintain complete and accurate records for all leads and opportunities.
  • Regular Pipeline Reviews: Conduct weekly reviews to update opportunity status and plan next steps.
  • Activity Tracking: Log all customer interactions to maintain relationship continuity and measure effort.
  • Follow-up Discipline: Set and honor follow-up commitments to maintain customer engagement.
  • Qualification Criteria: Establish clear lead qualification criteria to focus efforts on viable opportunities.
  • Competitive Intelligence: Maintain awareness of competitor activities and positioning.
  • Customer Feedback: Regularly seek feedback to improve sales process and customer experience.
Summary

The Sales Management module provides comprehensive tools for managing the entire customer acquisition process. From initial lead capture through opportunity development, proposal generation, and deal closure, the system supports sales teams in building relationships, managing pipelines, and achieving revenue targets through structured, data-driven sales processes.
