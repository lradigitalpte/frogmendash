Invoice Management

Complete Guide to Creating, Sending, and Managing Customer Invoices

Overview

The Invoice Management module provides comprehensive tools for creating professional invoices, tracking payments, managing customer billing, and maintaining financial records. Users can generate invoices from quotes or projects, customize invoice templates, automate recurring billing, and monitor accounts receivable.

This module integrates with sales, project management, and accounting systems to streamline the billing process from order to payment.

Create an Invoice

Steps to Create an Invoice

  1. Navigate to Billing → Invoices → New Invoice as shown in the below image.
    ![Create Invoice](images/billing/invoice_create.png)
  2. Select the customer and fill out invoice details.
    ![Invoice Form](images/billing/invoice_form.png)
  3. Add line items for products or services.
  4. Review totals and terms before saving.
  5. Click Save to create the invoice.

📋 Customer Information

Customer
Select the customer being billed.
Invoice Number
Unique identifier for the invoice (auto-generated).
Invoice Date
Date the invoice is issued.
Due Date
Payment due date.
Purchase Order Number
Customer's purchase order reference, if applicable. Important for customers requiring PO matching.
Project Reference
Link to specific project or work order. Enables project-based billing and cost tracking.

📦 Line Items

Product/Service
Select from products/services catalog or enter custom description. Include model numbers or specifications as needed.
Description
Detailed description of the item or service provided. Be specific to avoid customer queries.
Quantity
Number of units being billed. Include unit of measure (hours, pieces, licenses, etc.)
Unit Price
Price per unit before discounts. Should match quoted prices or catalog rates.
Discount (%)
Percentage discount applied to this line item, if applicable. Document reason for discounts.
Tax Rate
Applicable tax rate for this item. System can auto-apply based on customer location and product tax class.
Line Total
Automatically calculated as: (Quantity × Unit Price) - Discount + Tax.

Invoice Totals Section

Subtotal
Sum of all line items before discounts and taxes.
Total Discount
Combined discount amount across all line items.
Tax Total
Total tax amount across all applicable line items.
Invoice Total
Final amount due: Subtotal - Discounts + Taxes.
💡 Tip:
Double-check all amounts and customer information before sending to avoid payment delays and customer queries.

Converting Quotes to Invoices

Invoice from Accepted Quote

  1. Navigate to the accepted quote record.
    [Screenshot: Quote record showing Accepted status]
  2. Click Convert to Invoice button.
    [Screenshot: Convert to Invoice interface]
  3. Review and modify invoice details if needed.
  4. Adjust quantities or prices if partial delivery or changes occurred.
  5. Click Save to create the invoice.

Benefits of Quote-to-Invoice Conversion

  • Data Consistency: All product details, pricing, and customer information transfer automatically
  • Time Savings: Eliminates manual data entry and reduces errors
  • Audit Trail: Maintains link between original quote and final invoice
  • Price Protection: Preserves agreed pricing even if catalog prices change

Invoice Templates and Customization

Selecting Invoice Templates

Standard Template
Default company template with logo, standard layout, and payment terms.
Detailed Template
Extended format with additional product details, project information, and expanded terms.
Service Template
Optimized for service billing with time tracking, hourly rates, and professional services layout.
International Template
Multi-currency format with international address formats and tax compliance information.

Customizing Invoice Content

Payment Terms
Standard terms: "Payment due within 30 days of invoice date" or custom terms specific to customer agreements.
Payment Methods
Accepted payment options: Bank transfer, credit card, check, online portal. Include relevant account information.
Notes/Comments
Additional information for the customer: delivery instructions, warranty details, or project specifics.
Footer Information
Company registration details, tax numbers, contact information for billing inquiries.

Invoice Delivery Methods

Delivery Method When to Use Setup Requirements

Sending Invoice via Email

  1. From the invoice record, click Send Email.
    [Screenshot: Invoice email composition interface]
  2. Review email recipients and subject line.
  3. Customize email message if needed.
  4. Attach invoice PDF and any supporting documents.
  5. Click Send to deliver the invoice.

Email Template Customization

Email Subject
Professional subject line: "Invoice [Invoice Number] from [Company Name] - Due [Due Date]"
Email Body
Courteous message including payment instructions, due date, and contact information for questions.
Attachments
Invoice PDF, delivery receipts, contracts, or other supporting documentation.

Payment Tracking and Follow-up

Invoice Status Management

Status Description Typical Actions
Draft Invoice created but not yet finalized Review, edit, approve for sending
Sent Invoice delivered to customer Monitor for payment, schedule follow-up
Partial Payment Customer made partial payment Record payment, follow up on balance
Paid Invoice fully paid Record final payment, close invoice
Overdue Payment due date passed Send reminder, escalate collection efforts
Disputed Customer disputed invoice or amount Resolve dispute, adjust if necessary

Recording Payments

  1. From the invoice record, click Record Payment.
    [Screenshot: Payment recording interface]
  2. Enter payment details and amount received.
    [Screenshot: Payment entry form]
  3. Select payment method and reference information.
  4. Click Save to record the payment.

Payment Details

Payment Date
Date payment was received. Important for cash flow reporting and aging calculations.
Payment Amount
Amount received. Can be partial payment if customer paying in installments.
Payment Method
How payment was received: Bank transfer, credit card, check, cash, etc.
Reference Number
Transaction ID, check number, or other payment reference for reconciliation.
Payment Notes
Additional information about the payment or any special circumstances.

Recurring Invoices and Subscriptions

Setting Up Recurring Billing

  1. Create a template invoice with standard products/services.
    [Screenshot: Recurring invoice template setup]
  2. Configure recurrence schedule and parameters.
    [Screenshot: Recurrence configuration interface]
  3. Set start date and duration (if applicable).
  4. Activate recurring billing schedule.

Recurrence Configuration

Billing Frequency
How often invoices are generated:
  • Weekly: Every 7 days
  • Monthly: Same date each month
  • Quarterly: Every 3 months
  • Annually: Same date each year
  • Custom: Specific day intervals
Start Date
Date when recurring billing begins. First invoice will be generated on this date.
End Date
Optional end date for recurring series. Leave blank for ongoing subscriptions.
Auto-Send
Automatically email invoices to customer when generated, or create as draft for manual review.
Price Escalation
Optional annual price increases based on percentage or fixed amount.

Generating Invoice Reports

  1. Navigate to Invoices → Reports.
  2. Select desired report type and date range.
  3. Choose filtering options (customer, product, status).
  4. Generate report in PDF or Excel format.
  5. Schedule automated reports for regular distribution.

Use Cases & Examples

Professional Services Billing

Consulting firm billing for time and materials:

  • Time tracking: Capture hours worked by project and team member
  • Expense inclusion: Travel, materials, subcontractor costs
  • Billing rates: Different hourly rates by skill level and project type
  • Progress billing: Monthly invoices based on work completed
  • Project milestones: Billing tied to deliverable completion
  • Retainer management: Advance payments and draw-downs
  • Client reporting: Detailed breakdown of time and activities
Product Sales with Subscriptions

Software company with license sales and ongoing support:

  • Initial license sale: One-time invoice for software licenses
  • Implementation services: Professional services for setup and training
  • Monthly support: Recurring billing for ongoing support and maintenance
  • Usage-based billing: Additional charges based on transaction volume
  • Annual renewals: Subscription renewal invoices with price updates
  • Multi-currency: International customers billed in local currency
  • Volume discounts: Tiered pricing based on license count
Manufacturing and Distribution

Manufacturing company with complex product sales:

  • Product catalog: Standard pricing for manufactured goods
  • Custom configurations: Modified products with engineering charges
  • Volume pricing: Quantity breaks and contract pricing
  • Shipping charges: Freight and handling calculated by weight and distance
  • Payment terms: Net 30 for established customers, COD for new accounts
  • International trade: Export documentation and currency conversion
  • Warranty tracking: Extended warranty and service contract billing

Best Practices

  • Accurate Information: Verify all customer and product details before sending invoices to avoid disputes and delays.
  • Clear Descriptions: Use detailed, unambiguous descriptions for all products and services to prevent customer confusion.
  • Timely Billing: Send invoices promptly after delivery to maintain cash flow and customer expectations.
  • Consistent Terms: Apply payment terms consistently and communicate clearly with customers.
  • Follow-up Procedures: Establish systematic follow-up processes for overdue accounts.
  • Record Keeping: Maintain complete documentation for all invoices, payments, and customer communications.
  • Regular Reconciliation: Reconcile invoice records with accounting system and bank statements regularly.
Summary

The Invoice Management module provides comprehensive tools for professional billing and accounts receivable management. From creating accurate invoices and managing customer communications to tracking payments and generating financial reports, the system supports efficient billing operations that improve cash flow and customer relationships.
