Product Management

Complete Guide to Managing Product Catalog, Inventory, and Pricing

Overview

The Product Management module provides comprehensive tools for managing your product catalog, inventory levels, pricing strategies, and product information. Users can create detailed product records, track stock levels, manage variants and configurations, set pricing rules, and organize products into categories for easy browsing and sales.

This module integrates with sales, inventory, purchasing, and e-commerce systems to provide a central product information hub.

Adding New Products

Basic Product Information

Product Name
Clear, descriptive name for the product. Should be customer-facing and include key identifying features. Example: "Professional Wireless Headphones - Noise Canceling".
SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)
Unique internal identifier for inventory tracking. Use consistent naming convention (e.g., CAT-SUBCAT-MODEL-COLOR). Required for inventory and sales operations.
Product Description
Detailed description highlighting features, benefits, and specifications. Used in catalogs, quotes, and customer communications.
Short Description
Brief summary for use in product lists and quick references. Keep to 1-2 sentences.
Brand
Manufacturer or brand name. Used for filtering, reporting, and supplier management.
Product Category
Primary category classification for organization and filtering:
  • Electronics
  • Hardware
  • Software
  • Services
  • Consumables
  • Custom categories
Product Type
Classification affecting inventory and sales behavior:
  • Physical Product: Tangible goods requiring inventory tracking
  • Digital Product: Software, licenses, digital downloads
  • Service: Labor, consulting, support services
  • Subscription: Recurring service or software access

Product Specifications

Model Number
Manufacturer's model number or part number. Used for technical specifications and documentation.
UPC/Barcode
Universal Product Code for retail integration and inventory scanning.
Weight
Product weight for shipping calculations and logistics planning.
Dimensions
Length × Width × Height for storage and shipping requirements.
Color/Finish
Available colors or finishes. Can be managed as product variants.
Technical Specifications
Detailed technical specs in structured format. Include power requirements, compatibility, performance parameters.
💡 Tip:
Complete product specifications thoroughly as they're used in sales quotes, technical documentation, and customer support.

Product Variants and Configurations

Creating Product Variants

  1. From a product record, go to the Variants section.
    [Screenshot: Product variants management interface]
  2. Click + Add Variant to create a new configuration.
    [Screenshot: Variant creation form]
  3. Define variant attributes and unique characteristics.
  4. Set variant-specific pricing and inventory tracking.
  5. Click Save to create the variant.

Variant Attributes

Variant Name
Descriptive name combining base product with variant characteristics. Example: "Wireless Headphones - Black, Large".
Variant SKU
Unique SKU extending base product SKU. Example: "WH-NC-100-BLK-L".
Attribute Values
Specific values for variant dimensions:
  • Color: Black, White, Silver, Blue
  • Size: Small, Medium, Large, XL
  • Material: Aluminum, Plastic, Carbon Fiber
  • Configuration: Basic, Standard, Premium
Price Adjustment
Price difference from base product. Can be fixed amount (+$50) or percentage (+15%).
Availability
Control which variants are available for sale. Useful for discontinued colors or seasonal items.

Configurable Products

For complex products with many customization options:

  • Define configuration rules and constraints
  • Set up option groups (Hardware, Software, Support)
  • Create pricing rules for option combinations
  • Establish lead times for custom configurations

Pricing Management

Setting Product Pricing

Cost Price
Your cost to acquire or manufacture the product. Used for profit calculations and margin reporting.
List Price
Standard retail price before discounts. Often used as baseline for discount calculations.
Sale Price
Current selling price to customers. Can be different from list price due to promotions or market conditions.
Minimum Price
Lowest price allowed for sales. Protects profit margins and prevents deep discounting.

Tiered Pricing

Quantity Breaks
Volume-based pricing tiers:
  • 1-9 units: $100 each
  • 10-49 units: $95 each
  • 50+ units: $90 each
Customer Group Pricing
Different prices for customer segments:
  • Retail customers: List price
  • Wholesale customers: 20% discount
  • Partners/Resellers: 35% discount
  • VIP customers: Special negotiated rates

Currency and International Pricing

Multi-Currency Support
Set prices in multiple currencies for international sales. System can auto-convert or use fixed exchange rates.
Regional Pricing
Different pricing by geographic region to account for local market conditions, taxes, and costs.

Inventory Management

Stock Level Tracking

Current Stock
Current quantity on hand across all warehouses/locations. Updated automatically with sales and receipts.
Reserved Stock
Quantity committed to pending orders but not yet shipped. Reduces available stock for new orders.
Available Stock
Current Stock minus Reserved Stock. Available for new orders and quotations.
Reorder Level
Stock level that triggers reorder notification. Set based on lead times and consumption patterns.
Reorder Quantity
Standard quantity to order when restocking. Optimized for order minimums and storage capacity.

Inventory Tracking Methods

Method Best For Characteristics
Perpetual High-value items, fast-moving inventory Real-time tracking, automatic updates from transactions
Periodic Low-value consumables, slow-moving items Manual counts at regular intervals
Just-in-Time Custom/made-to-order products Minimal stock, ordered based on customer demand
Non-Tracked Digital products, unlimited services No quantity limitations or stock tracking

Multi-Location Inventory

Warehouse Allocation
Track inventory across multiple storage locations. Enable location-specific sales and transfers.
Location Priority
Define fulfillment priorities by location (closest to customer, lowest shipping cost, fastest processing).

Image Guidelines

Primary Image
Main product photo used in catalogs and listings. Should be clear, well-lit, and show product from best angle.
Additional Views
Multiple angles, close-ups of features, scale references, and usage scenarios.
Technical Diagrams
Dimensional drawings, exploded views, installation diagrams for technical products.
Lifestyle Images
Products in use, showing scale and application context.

Product Lifecycle Management

Lifecycle Stages

Stage Characteristics Actions
Development Product being designed and tested Create preliminary records, gather specifications
Introduction New product launch Marketing materials, pricing strategy, initial inventory
Growth Increasing demand and sales Scale inventory, optimize pricing, expand variants
Maturity Stable sales, market saturation Maintain inventory, competitive pricing, cost optimization
Decline Decreasing demand Reduce inventory, clearance pricing, plan replacement
Discontinued No longer sold Clear remaining inventory, support existing customers

Product Status Management

Active
Available for sale and quotation. Full inventory and pricing management.
Inactive
Temporarily unavailable. Hidden from catalogs but records maintained.
Discontinued
No longer available for new sales. Support and documentation remain accessible.

Use Cases & Examples

Technology Hardware Retailer

Electronics store managing diverse product catalog:

  • Product categories: Computers, tablets, smartphones, accessories
  • Variant management: Different storage sizes, colors, and configurations
  • Supplier relationships: Multiple brands and manufacturers
  • Inventory tracking: Real-time stock levels across store locations
  • Pricing strategy: Competitive pricing with margin protection
  • Seasonal adjustments: Holiday pricing and clearance campaigns
  • Product lifecycle: Regular introduction of new models, discontinuation of old ones
Manufacturing Company

Industrial equipment manufacturer with custom configurations:

  • Base products: Standard equipment models with core specifications
  • Configurable options: Power ratings, control systems, mounting configurations
  • Engineering drawings: Technical documentation and installation guides

Software and Services Company

Technology company offering software and professional services:

  • Subscription models: Monthly and annual recurring billing
  • Professional services: Implementation, training, and support packages
  • Digital delivery: Download links and license key management
  • Version management: Software updates and backward compatibility
  • Bundle packages: Combined software and services offerings
  • Usage-based pricing: Metered billing for transaction-based services

Best Practices

  • Consistent Naming: Use clear, consistent naming conventions for products, SKUs, and categories.
  • Complete Information: Maintain thorough product descriptions, specifications, and documentation.
  • Regular Updates: Keep pricing, inventory, and status information current and accurate.
  • Quality Images: Use high-resolution, professional product photography and technical diagrams.
  • Logical Organization: Structure categories and attributes to match customer browsing patterns.
  • Lifecycle Management: Regularly review product performance and lifecycle status.
  • Data Governance: Establish clear workflows for product data creation, updates, and approval.
Summary

The Product Management module provides comprehensive tools for managing your complete product catalog. From detailed product records and variant management to pricing strategies and inventory tracking, the system supports effective product lifecycle management that drives sales, maintains customer satisfaction, and optimizes business operations.
